    Shared node · Failing

    Cells switch to anaerobic metabolism

    Energy production collapses and acid accumulates.

    What is actually happening

    Without oxygen, cells can only extract about two ATP per glucose instead of roughly thirty-six, and lactic acid accumulates as a byproduct. Falling pH impairs enzyme function, reduces cardiac contractility, and blunts the response to catecholamines.

    Why it follows from almost anything

    It is the cellular consequence of sustained hypoxia from any cause, which is why this node is shared by respiratory failure, shock, and cardiac arrest alike.

    What you see

    • Metabolic acidosis, which the body tries to buffer by breathing faster and deeper
    • Progressive failure of compensation as acidosis blunts the sympathetic response
    • Cool, mottled skin and prolonged capillary refill
    The trap

    This is the point where the cascade becomes self-reinforcing. Acidosis reduces cardiac output, which worsens delivery, which worsens acidosis. Once the loop closes, correcting the original cause alone may no longer be enough.
