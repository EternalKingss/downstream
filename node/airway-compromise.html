    Airway stops protecting itself

    Muscle tone goes, the tongue falls back, and reflexes stop working.

    What is actually happening

    Consciousness maintains pharyngeal muscle tone and the gag and cough reflexes. As consciousness falls, the tongue loses tone and falls against the posterior pharyngeal wall, and the patient loses the ability to clear secretions, blood, or vomit.

    Why it follows from almost anything

    It is a direct mechanical consequence of falling level of consciousness, whatever caused that fall.

    What you see

    • Snoring or gurgling respirations
    • Paradoxical or see-saw chest movement with no air movement
    • Absent gag reflex
    • Silence, which in a previously noisy airway means complete obstruction, not improvement
    The trap

    This is a second, independent lethal problem stacked on top of the original one. A patient can survive their overdose and die of their airway. It is also the one thing on this entire site that is fixed by positioning alone, at no cost, in under five seconds.
