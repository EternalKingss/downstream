    Medications

    Every drug is filed by the link in the chain it interrupts. Side effects are treated as the same mechanism acting somewhere you did not want it, because that is what they are.

    OxygenRaises the pressure gradient driving oxygen across the alveolar membrane. It does not fix anything upstream of that.SalbutamolRelaxes bronchial smooth muscle. It reverses spasm and nothing else.EpinephrineHits alpha and beta receptors at once, which is why it can reverse an entire anaphylactic cascade in one dose.NaloxonePhysically displaces opioids from their receptors, switching the respiratory drive back on.Acetylsalicylic acidPermanently disables the platelets that are building a clot in a coronary artery.NitroglycerinDilates veins so less blood returns to the heart, which cuts the work the heart has to do.Oral glucosePuts fuel back into a brain that has run out and cannot make its own.
