    Medication · Non-selective adrenergic agonist

    Epinephrine (Adrenaline)

    Hits alpha and beta receptors at once, which is why it can reverse an entire anaphylactic cascade in one dose.

    EMR  Auto-injector administration for anaphylaxis is widely within EMR scope. Drawn-up IM epinephrine and any IV use are not. Verify against your service's protocol.

    Breaks the chain at

    Airway narrows three ways

    Mechanism

    Epinephrine is non-selective, and in anaphylaxis that is precisely the point. Alpha-1 stimulation constricts blood vessels, which raises blood pressure and reduces the mucosal swelling closing the airway. Beta-1 stimulation increases heart rate and contractility. Beta-2 stimulation relaxes bronchial smooth muscle and stabilises mast cells so they stop releasing more mediators. One drug interrupts the cascade at four separate points simultaneously.

    Why it works at that link and not another

    Compare it to salbutamol, which only touches beta-2. In anaphylaxis the patient is not just bronchospastic, they are also vasodilated, leaking fluid into tissue, and swelling shut. Salbutamol addresses one of those. Epinephrine addresses all of them, and it is the only drug that does.

    Dosing

    RouteDoseNotes
    IM, adult0.3 to 0.5 mg of 1:1000Anterolateral thigh. Repeat every 5 to 15 minutes if the response is inadequate.
    IM, paediatric0.01 mg/kg, maximum 0.3 mgAuto-injectors come as 0.15 mg for roughly 10 to 25 kg and 0.3 mg above that.

    Target: Reversal of airway swelling, improving blood pressure, and resolution of bronchospasm.

    Timing

    This is the part that tells you when to reassess, and it is the part most people never learn properly.

    Onset
    3 to 5 minutes when given IM into the thigh.
    Peak
    Around 10 minutes.
    Duration
    10 to 20 minutes only. This is the number people forget.

    What working looks like

    • Stridor and airway swelling settle
    • Blood pressure rises
    • Wheeze improves
    • Urticaria begins to fade, usually last

    What it means if nothing happens

    Repeat the dose. Under-dosing and delayed dosing cause far more harm in anaphylaxis than the drug itself does. If there is still no response, consider that the diagnosis may not be anaphylaxis.

    Cautions

    There is no absolute contraindication in true anaphylaxis

    The risk of withholding it exceeds the risk of giving it, including in patients with cardiac disease. Hesitation kills more anaphylaxis patients than epinephrine ever has.

    Known cardiac disease or severe hypertension

    Increased rate and contractility raise myocardial oxygen demand, and vasoconstriction raises afterload. Worth knowing, not a reason to withhold.

    Side effects are the drug doing its job elsewhere

    Tachycardia and palpitations are beta-1 doing its job. Tremor and anxiety are beta-2 and central sympathetic effects. Pallor is alpha-1 vasoconstriction in the skin. Headache is vascular. Every one of these is the drug working correctly, and their presence is reassurance that the dose was actually delivered.
