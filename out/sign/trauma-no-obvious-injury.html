    Shock with no visible injury

    If they are shocked and there is nothing on the outside, the blood is on the inside.

    What produces it, and how to tell them apart

    Ordered roughly by how quickly each will kill. The clue is what separates it from the others on this list.

    Solid organ injury

    Blunt mechanism, lower rib fractures, seat belt marks, left shoulder tip pain.

    Hemothorax

    Unilateral reduced breath sounds with dullness, flat neck veins.

    Fractures

    Femur can lose one to two litres, pelvis several. Multiple long bones alone can cause shock.

    Ruptured abdominal aortic aneurysm

    Over 50, severe back or flank pain, often a soft abdomen because the blood is retroperitoneal.

    Gastrointestinal bleeding

    Melaena or haematemesis may not yet have appeared, so ask about black stools and dizziness.

    Obstetric hemorrhage

    Placental abruption conceals blood behind the placenta. Rigid painful uterus with shock and little visible blood.

    Cardiac tamponade

    Distended neck veins with equal breath sounds after penetrating chest trauma.

    Tension pneumothorax

    Distended neck veins with unilaterally absent breath sounds.

    Neurogenic shock

    Warm, dry, pink, bradycardic. Assume bleeding first and treat accordingly.

    Test yourself

    First moves

    • Assume internal bleeding and treat as major hemorrhage.
    • Check breath sounds bilaterally to exclude a chest source.
    • Palpate the abdomen gently and note tenderness or distension, then reassess later for change.
    • Consider the pelvis by mechanism, apply a binder if indicated, and do not spring it to test.
    • Warm aggressively, minimise scene time, and pre-alert a trauma centre.

    What this sign actually means

    Five spaces can each hold enough blood to kill: the chest, the abdomen, the retroperitoneum, the pelvis, and the thighs. Plus the floor, which people forget to look at. None of them will look obviously distended until far too late, and none of them can be compressed.
