    Circulatory system

    Move oxygenated blood to tissue and bring it back.

    Where things go wrong

    Every condition below is filed under the part it physically damages. Read down the list and notice that the groups are really a list of the ways this system can fail — and that knowing which group you are in tells you most of what you need before you have a diagnosis.

    Myocardium

    Failure mode: The pump can't generate output

    Acute coronary syndromeSudden cardiac arrestCardiogenic shockCardiac tamponadeCongestive heart failureStimulant toxicity

    Coronary arteries

    Failure mode: The pump's own blood supply is cut

    Acute coronary syndrome

    Conduction system

    Failure mode: Electrical timing breaks down

    Sudden cardiac arrestCardiogenic shockElectrical injury

    Heart valves

    Failure mode: Blood goes the wrong way or can't get out

    Congestive heart failure

    Pericardium

    Failure mode: The heart can't fill

    Cardiac tamponade

    Arteries and arterioles

    Failure mode: Pressure can't be maintained, or the route is blocked

    External hemorrhageSeptic shockNeurogenic shockTraumatic amputationAnaphylaxis

    Capillaries

    Failure mode: Exchange fails, or fluid leaks out

    Hemorrhagic and hypovolemic shockSeptic shockThe deteriorating childAnaphylaxis

    Veins

    Failure mode: Blood can't get back to the heart

    External hemorrhageHemorrhagic and hypovolemic shockNeurogenic shock

    Blood

    Failure mode: Nothing there to carry the oxygen

    Gastrointestinal bleedingExternal hemorrhageHemorrhagic and hypovolemic shockHemothoraxSmoke inhalationCarbon monoxide poisoning
